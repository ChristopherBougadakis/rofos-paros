Pirate’s Cove

Tour stop 02

A hidden, historic coastal cave

The Pirate’s Cave (or Cave of Panteronisi) in Paros is a hidden, historic coastal cave near the southern tip of the island, best accessed by boat trips from Aliki or Pounta. Famous for its turquoise waters, snorkeling opportunities, and, according to local legend, a former pirate refuge, it is a popular spot for half-day tours.

Experience

The area is known for its shallow, crystal-clear blue and white waters. The cave itself offers a dramatic, rocky entrance often surrounded by, or located near, spots like the “Blue Lagoon” or “Aspra Nera”.

Activities

The location is ideal for snorkeling, cliff jumping from safe heights and a unique chance to take some amazing pictures through the rocky archways.

Atmosphere

A “Hidden Gem” vibe that feels secluded and adventurous.
