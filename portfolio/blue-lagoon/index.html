Blue Lagoon

Tour stop 01

Dive into the Turquoise Paradise of the Blue Lagoon

Experience the most exotic side of the Aegean. Nestled between Paros and Antiparos, the Panteronisia islets form a breathtaking ‘Blue Lagoon’ with neon-turquoise waters and a bright white sandy seabed. Accessible only by boat, this hidden gem feels like a natural swimming pool in the middle of the ocean. Join us for a journey to crystal-clear depths, where you can swim, snorkel, and soak in the Mediterranean sun far from the crowds.
