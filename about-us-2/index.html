Our history

Rofos was launched in 2012 with the aim of offering a unique experience in Paros. Starting with our first boat, measuring 11.5 meters in length and 3.3 meters in width, and with unlimited passion and a strong desire to make everyone who boards our boat feel the same love for the sea that we have.

And today

With all the love and support we received from you, in 2017 we decided to renovate and blend the Greek tradition of wooden caiques with our passion for providing the best experience to our clients. With the help of Mr. Lekkas’ shipyard, which had already built over 170 wooden boats, we began constructing a larger, more comfortable vessel, with every small detail crafted by hand. Measuring 14.5 meters in length and 5 meters in width, the new ship incorporates all the traditional features of a wooden ‘trechantiri’ (as this shape of wooden caique is known), while offering modern amenities to ensure the best experience during our tours.
