FAQ

Yes, both of our daily tours are exactly the same in both what we offer and the spots that we visit! The choice should only be based on what fits your schedule the most.

In the main website page there are book now buttons that transfers you to a safe booking website. Then you just click on your preferred time and date and place the number of adults/children that will join us. With very easy payment options you will finalise your booking. After completing the steps there should be a confirmation email sent to you with all the details that you chose!

Yes of course, we will be very happy to host any event that you may like from weddings, private excursions of either 4 or 7 hours or even a relaxing sunset cruise! Just send us the idea, the number of guests and the date through email and we will answer very shortly with a quote. Also until 14 guests we do have our new sailing boat pearl that can offer private tours for groups until that number.

Especially in the high season there are a lot of times that we reach our maximum guests so we can’t have more bookings at the exact time and date. You could check the availability of other days that do fit your schedule!

We have set a maximum of 40 people on board even the boat was made for more so everyone can have their own place to relax, place their stuff and enjoy the cruise!

As everywhere the earliest is always the best to ensure that you will be at your exact preferred time and date. Especially for the high season it’s recommended to book at least a week in advance!

We do offer drinks, fruits and local delicacies throughout the duration of the trip. Our drinks are water, sparkling beverages (orange, lemon, cola, sprite), Greek beers and white wine from our local winery of Moraitis in Naousa, Paros! To ensure a smooth trip for everyone we limit only our alcoholic drinks to two per person so we avoid any unnecessary dizziness!

Yes you can be 100% refunded if you notify us more than 48 hours before the trip that you booked. For less than 48 hours unfortunately there will be charges and the last 24 hours or for the no shows there isn’t any refund.

Unfortunately weather is not always with our side so if the experienced team of our boat comes to the conclusion that the weather is not as it should be for you to enjoy our tour then there will be an email sent from us to rebook your ticket any time and date that is available or fully refund you!

Our boat is at the small port of aliki in south Paros, Greece in front of the traditional restaurant named “Aliki”. You can easily see the exact location just by clicking on the “get directions” button in the confirmation email that has been sent to you when you did the booking!
