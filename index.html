Our daily schedule

01 30 min

Blue Lagoon

Our first stop is the wonderful Panteronissi with its turquoise waters. Enjoy the sun and swim in the sea, with or without a mask offered by our staff — fresh, summer fruit and drinks served onboard.

→
02 1 hour

Pirate’s Cove

We anchor at the magical Pirate’s Cove for an hour. Explore the cave and its deep waters with masks provided by us, with freshly made traditional snacks, drinks and refreshments.

→
03 Photo stop

Tripiti Cave

Upon departure, we take a short break near the stunning cave of Tripiti — the perfect moment for photos through the rocky archways.

→
04 30 min

St. Myron

Our third stop is at Agios Miron (St. Myron), where guests can enjoy a final swim in crystal clear waters before returning to the port of Alyki.

→

Depending on weather conditions, the Captain may suggest an alternate route to ensure a safe and enjoyable journey. Vegetarian options can be included with notification but not vegan. Please notify us for any allergy!
